Pocket Nutrition is the native Android client for the Pocket Nutrition project. Search for an ingredient, set a portion and a cooking method, analyse it, and save the result to a local food journal. The interface is available in French and English.

Nutritional values come from the Pocket Nutrition API, which predicts how cooking changes a food's profile and is checked against public reference data. Product photos and data come from Open Food Facts. You can point the app at your own backend server in Settings.

You can also log a meal by voice: the recording is sent to a recognition server that you configure yourself (this feature is off until you do), and the clip is deleted from your phone once the result is back.

The journal lives on your device. The app asks for the camera only to scan barcodes, for the microphone only while you record a meal, and for network access to reach the API.

Pocket Nutrition is free software, released under the MPL-2.0 licence. Source and issue tracker: https://github.com/pocket-nutrition/android
